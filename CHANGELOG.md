# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-06

### Added

- Change the volume by scrolling over a video, in steps from 1% to 20%.
- Fine 1% steps below 10% volume.
- Activation always, while holding Shift, or while holding the right mouse button.
- Volume boost up to 150%, 200% or 300%, highlighted in red above 100%.
- Minimal volume indicator at the top or center of the player.
- Middle click on the player to mute or unmute.
- Automatic video quality from 360p up to 4K or the highest available, falling back to the next lower quality.
- Support for regular videos, Shorts, YouTube Music and embedded players.
- Settings popup with sync across devices.
- Support for Firefox and Chromium-based browsers from a single code base.
