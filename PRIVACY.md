# Privacy Policy

**Volume Scroll and Auto HD for YouTube** does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

## What the extension stores

Your settings, such as the step size or the preferred quality, are saved with the browser's extension storage (`storage.sync`). If browser sync is turned on, your browser may sync these settings between your own devices. The developer has no access to them.

To keep your volume after a page reload, the extension writes the current volume to the same local storage entry that YouTube itself uses for this purpose. It never leaves your browser.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, telemetry or tracking
- No network requests of its own
- No remote code
- No reading of your browsing history, search queries, comments or account data

## Volume boost

Volume boost above 100% processes the video's audio locally in your browser using the Web Audio API. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere.

## Permissions

| Permission | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Saves your settings. |
| Access to `youtube.com`, `music.youtube.com` and `youtube-nocookie.com` | Required to control the video player on these sites. |
